# Campus photographs

`doug-duck.png` is a transparent cutout of the user's original `Desktop/40 Archive/41 Archived Projects/13 P.M./Doug the Duck/Drawings/Doug Outline.jpg`, prepared with image generation to remove the exterior background and corner artifacts. The site's PNG favicons and touch icon are resized derivatives of the cutout.

`doug-wave-0.png` through `doug-wave-3.png` are transparent pose cutouts prepared with image generation from the four frames of the user's `Downloads/Untitled_Artwork.gif`, cropped to matching square canvases. Pose 0 is the static wing-down greeting; the other poses form the five-wave hover sequence. The active `doug-rest-*` favicons, touch icon, and `favicon.ico`/`favicon.svg` fallbacks use pose 0.

- `campus-uva.webp`: “Rotunda - University of Virginia,” Robert Emerson. [Original photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rotunda_-_University_of_Virginia.jpg). [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). This WebP derivative retains that license.
- `campus-wisconsin.webp`: “Bascom Hall (6933616835),” Richard Hurd. [Original photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bascom_Hall_(6933616835).jpg). [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/).

Both photographs were resized to 960px wide and converted to WebP. These retained assets are currently unused; the education tile now uses original book illustrations.

Product screenshots and the personal photographs/video predate this artwork update. The route, envelope, browser frames, and track lines are original HTML/CSS/SVG artwork.

`tfrrs-placeholder.svg` is an original labeled preview placeholder. The previous TFRRS Monitor PNG screenshots and WebP derivatives were removed at the user's request.

`syllabus.webp` (and `../projects/syllabus-calendar.webp`, `../projects/syllabus-grading.webp`) are screenshots of the live Syllabus Analyzer's built-in CS571 demo, captured October 7, 2026. `time.webp` is a screenshot of the live Time Progress Visualizer's Year view, captured the same day; it replaces an earlier generated mockup.

`coder-card-octocat.webp` and `coder-card-karpathy.webp` were resized and converted from the user's Desktop BrainForge Coder Card media collection. `neon-site-current.webp` was resized and converted from the user's supplied Neon.ai screenshot dated October 3, 2026.

## US map

The map geometry in `src/data/usMap.ts` comes from [us-atlas states-albers-10m.json](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/us-atlas@3/states-albers-10m.json), a redistribution of the US Census Bureau's cartographic boundaries in the Albers USA projection. State shapes are converted to SVG paths; the route and location markers are original overlays.
