The Harmony of Body, Intellect, and Soul

History 201 - 2025 14 pages

This paper analyzes how the Nazi regime utilized the 1936 Berlin Olympics as a sophisticated propaganda tool to manufacture an image of a modern, peaceful Germany. By examining the strategic manipulation of urban infrastructure, media technology, and temporary shifts in social policy, the study demonstrates how the spectacle was designed to mask authoritarian oppression and secure international legitimacy.

A Heresy of Blood: The Transition from Faith to Race in Early Modern Spain

History 600 - 2025 20 pages

This research challenges the traditional religious narrative of the Spanish Inquisition, arguing instead that it functioned primarily as a political weapon of the State to enforce racial homogeneity. By tracing the shift from theological heresy to the doctrine of limpieza de sangre (blood purity), the paper demonstrates how the Catholic Monarchs nationalized the tribunal to target the converso class not for what they believed, but for who they were.
